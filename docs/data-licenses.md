# Data licenses and attributions

Every dataset the `vibeglobe-data` pipeline of `@vibeglobe/tools` builds with its shipped configurations, and the optional fresher Sentinel-2 mosaic: its license, the exact attribution its outputs carry, its source and the day its terms were read. Every output carries the same fields in its metadata (`metadata.json`, the PMTiles metadata, the MBTiles metadata table).

The application hosts these outputs itself and shows each attribution wherever the data is displayed. The online sources of the Europe preset are not pipeline outputs: `europeOpen(…).sources` lists them with their terms ([Europe preset](guides/europe-preset.md)).

## Refused sources

The pipeline and the offline package builder (`vibeglobe-data pack`) refuse these before downloading anything:

| Source                            | Why                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sentinel-2 cloudless by EOX       | The 2018 and later editions are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial); the 2016 edition needs EOX's licence for commercial use |
| Public OpenStreetMap tile servers | Their usage policy forbids bulk downloading, tile archives and offline use                                                |
| Google Maps Platform tiles        | Its terms forbid pre-fetching, caching outside the allowed temporary cache, and redistribution                            |

`vibeglobe-data licenses` lists every built-in provider profile and whether its terms allow packaging.
