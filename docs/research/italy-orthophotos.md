# Free colour orthophotos for Italy: commercial use, redistribution and Web Mercator access (research, 2026-10-28)

Criterion: free colour imagery usable online to Web Mercator z15–16 (browser fetches tiles), with terms that allow commercial use AND redistribution (an offline package counts as redistribution). Conflicting or unstated terms fail.

**Access caveat (read first).** No provider host could be reached from this container. `curl` got a 403 from the egress proxy for every Italian host tried (geoportale.piemonte.it, geomap.reteunitaria.piemonte.it, opengis.csi.it, wms.pcn.minambiente.it, servizigis.regione.emilia-romagna.it, geoservices.buergernetz.bz.it, siat.provincia.tn.it, idt2.regione.veneto.it, tiles.maps.eox.at, s2maps.eu, inspire-geoportal.ec.europa.eu, dati.gov.it, geodati.gov.it, …). WebFetch could not resolve those hosts either, nor the trade-press hosts (rivistageomedia.it, gisinfrastrutture.it). So:

- every licence statement below comes from **web-search quotes of the provider's own pages or records**, and is marked "not verified at source";
- **CORS is "unknown" for every service**, because no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` header could be checked;
- endpoints and CRS lists were cross-checked against the OSM editor-layer-index (`osmlab/editor-layer-index`, cloned 2026-10-28). That index was used for endpoints and CRS only, never for licences.

Zoom formula: z ≈ log2(156543·cos(lat)/res). At 45° N: 0.2 m → z19.1, 0.3 m → z18.5, 0.45 m → z17.9, 1 m → z16.8, 2 m → z15.8, 10 m → z13.4.

## 4. Fails the criterion, and why

- **Piemonte AGEA 2015/2018/2021/2024 WMS/WMTS (opengis.csi.it)**: the regional record says "(c) tutti i diritti riservati" or leaves the terms unknown. It conflicts with AGEA's national CC BY 4.0, so the terms conflict.
- **Piemonte ICE 2009–2011**: CC BY 2.5 vs 4.0 on ARPA's mirror, no licence in the owner's record, 15 years old.
- **Torino city 2023, CMTO 2010**: terms unknown, or partial coverage and a private producer.
- **AGEA GeoPortale 2022–2024**: CC BY 4.0 is reported, but there is no WMS/WMTS/XYZ, downloads are capped, and the T&C were not read. Fails online; a candidate for a self-hosted package.
- **PCN / MASE 1988–2012**: the MASE FAQ excludes orthophotos from CC BY 4.0, records conflict, and the imagery is old.
- **Copernicus VHR 2018/2021**: restricted, view-only; 2 m.
- **EOX s2cloudless 2016**: conflicting (CC BY 4.0 on pricing vs a commercial licence with no redistribution on the licence page); 10 m.
- **EOX 2018–2025**: CC BY-NC-SA. **Esri / Google / Bing**: proprietary, keys, no caching or redistribution.
- **Terrascope WorldCover**: service terms unclear (existing exclusion); 10 m, so about z13–14 at most.
- **Lombardia, Veneto, Valle d'Aosta, Sardegna, Toscana 2019, E-R AGEA 2020/2023, Trentino AGEA 2023**: AGEA institutional licences; commercial use barred or reproduction forbidden.
- **Toscana 2023**: CC BY 4.0 vs CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 and "titolarità della Regione".
- **Trentino 2015, FVG 2017–20, Liguria, Puglia, Sicilia, Lazio, Umbria, Marche, Campania**: no official licence found, or no open licence.
